About the Data: Alibaba DLRM Trace

Derived from Alibaba's production environment, this dataset captures the operational characteristics of over 23,000 latency-sensitive inference instances.

It covers both CPU-only (CN) and Heterogeneous GPU (HN) nodes, providing a rare opportunity to analyze resource saturation, scheduling bottlenecks, and long-tail latency in large-scale AI serving infrastructure.

* Citation: Alibaba Cluster Trace Program, "GPU-Disaggregated Inference Cluster (DLRM Trace)", NSDI '25.
